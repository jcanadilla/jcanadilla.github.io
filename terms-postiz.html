NUBO Postiz terms

What NUBO Postiz is, who uses it and what for: an internal tool I use to publish Nubo's content to its official accounts.

Last updated: 05.11.2026 / Javier Cañadilla / Leer en español
01

Who is behind this

NUBO Postiz belongs to Javier Cañadilla Casco, an independent developer acting as an individual (there is no company behind it). You can write to me at javier@javiercanadilla.com with any question about these terms.

02

What NUBO Postiz is

An internal tool, hosted at pz.javiercanadilla.com, that only I use to publish Nubo's own content to the game's official accounts (@nubopuzzle on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram, and its Facebook page) through those platforms' official APIs.

03

How it is used

NUBO Postiz is not a public service. It has no sign-up, it is not used by third parties and it does not post on behalf of anyone else: only I use it, with my own accounts, to schedule and publish Nubo videos and images that I created.

  • It does not collect or store data from other TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or Facebook users.
  • It only accesses Nubo's official accounts, with the permissions I grant it myself, and I can revoke them at any time from each platform.
  • The content it publishes follows each platform's rules, and its use is also subject to the terms of TikTok, YouTube (Google), Instagram and Facebook (Meta).

The terms for the game Nubo are in the games terms of service, and data processing is covered in the privacy policy.

04

Changes and governing law

If the way the tool is used changes, I will update this page and the date at the top. These terms are governed by Spanish law and are also available in Spanish; if the two versions differ, the Spanish one prevails.
